### [If You are Asking for Human Attention, Demonstrate Human Effort](https://tombedor.dev/human-attention-and-human-effort/)

It's rude to ask other folks to read and parse what your <span class="emoji">🤖</span> spews out when you haven't read it yourself, let alone *thought* it through. I've seen it happen. But most of the time, folks on my team edit, fix, and put in effort before passing along AI-generated output.
